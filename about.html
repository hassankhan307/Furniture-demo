About NESTORA

Furniture With Purpose

NESTORA began with a simple frustration: too much furniture available in Pakistan was either mass-produced and forgettable, or beautifully designed and out of reach. We set out to close that gap.

Our story

Started in a small Karachi workshop.

NESTORA started with three carpenters and one small workshop in Karachi. What began as custom orders for friends and family grew into a full collection — built the same way, one piece at a time, with the same attention to how furniture actually lives in a home.

Our mission

Good design shouldn't be a luxury import.

Our mission is to make thoughtfully designed, well-built furniture accessible to modern Pakistani households — without shipping it from overseas or compromising on materials.

Quality & Craftsmanship

Every NESTORA piece passes through the same three checkpoints before it reaches your home.

Materials

Sourced, not settled for

Solid Sheesham, oak and walnut, paired with fabrics chosen for how they hold up to daily life, not just how they photograph.

Construction

Built by hand, checked by hand

Every frame is joined and finished by our in-house carpenters, then inspected before it's approved for delivery.

Comfort

Tested for real rooms

We sit in it, sleep on it, and live with every prototype before it becomes part of the collection.

Modern Pakistani homes

Designed for the way homes here actually work.

Joint families, smaller apartments, big get-togethers, humid summers — our pieces are designed and tested with the realities of Pakistani homes in mind, not adapted from a catalog built for somewhere else.
